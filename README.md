# 🐸 Ribbits

Tiny swamp friends, cozy frog villages, soft decorations, little bands, trading, and a whole lot of ribbit-shaped charm.

**Ribbits** adds adorable frog-inspired creatures that live in swamp villages. They wander around mushroomy homes, play music together, trade with players, care for crops, fish by the water, and make swamps feel warmer, livelier, and a little more magical.

> 🌿 This fork contains an **unofficial Fabric 26.1.x port**. The original mod, concept, art, design, and creative work belong to the original authors credited below.

## 🐸 Creatures

**Ribbits** are small, expressive creatures with different professions, models, and behaviors.

- 🌱 **Nitwit Ribbit**: a silly little friend who can join Ribbit bands.
- 🎣 **Fisherman Ribbit**: hangs around water, fishes, and offers fish-related trades.
- 🌾 **Gardener Ribbit**: helps nearby crops grow with a watering animation.
- 💎 **Merchant Ribbit**: trades decorations, blocks, and items from the mod.
- ✨ **Sorcerer Ribbit**: casts helpful magic that grants positive effects to nearby players.

They can also hold umbrellas in the rain, float gently while falling, return home at night, and move around their villages like tiny swamp residents.

## 🎶 Interactions

Ribbits is more than decoration: it adds playful little moments to the world.

- 🎵 **Ribbit bands**: Ribbits can gather and play bass, bongos, flute, or guitar.
- 🪇 **Maraca**: players can use a maraca to join the music.
- 💎 **Trading**: Merchant and Fisherman Ribbits trade items using amethyst shards.
- 🌾 **Crop care**: Gardener Ribbits can water and grow nearby crops.
- ✨ **Support magic**: Sorcerer Ribbits can grant regeneration, resistance, strength, jump boost, haste, or health boost.
- 🏡 **Home spot**: sneak-use an amethyst shard on a Ribbit to update its home position.
- 🤝 **Vanilla-friendly trading**: trading Ribbits stay still and look at the player while the trade UI is open, just like Minecraft merchants.

## 🏡 Ribbit Villages

The heart of the mod is its villages. These tiny communities generate naturally in the world and include:

- 🍄 Mushroom-style houses
- 🛶 Swamp-friendly paths and bridges
- 🏮 Lamp posts and decorative details
- 🐸 Ribbits with different professions
- 🌿 Custom vegetation and modded blocks

In this Fabric 26.1.x port, the required YUNG-style generation logic is kept locally so Ribbit villages can continue to work without depending on an external YUNG's API release that does not yet exist for this Minecraft version.
